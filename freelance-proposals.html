20 Freelance Proposals (fill-in)

Replace [brackets]. Lead with proof, offer a paid trial.

1 · First contact (Upwork)

"Hi [Name], I saw your post for [task]. I've delivered [result] for [client type] and can turn around [deliverable] in [time]. Daily async updates. 2 examples attached. I can start a paid 1-day trial."

2 · Fiverr gig description

"I will [specific deliverable] in [timeframe] using [tool/method]. Includes [A, B, C]. Revisions: [n]. Delivery: [n] days. Message before ordering for custom scope."

3 · Response to "what's your rate?"

"For this scope $[X] fixed or $[/hr]/hr. Includes 1 revision + 24h start. I can do a paid trial first."

4 · Trial offer

"To remove risk, I'll do the first [deliverable] as a paid trial at $[low]. If it's not what you need, you don't continue."

5 · After no reply (4 days)

"Hi [Name] — circling back. I can take [task] off your plate this week. Want a 1-page plan so you can decide fast?"

6 · Scope confirmation

"Confirming: deliverable [Y], timeline [Z], price $[X], 1 revision. I start within 24h of approval."

7 · Mid-project update

"Day 2 of [project]: [done] complete, [next] in progress, on track for [date]. Sample attached."

8 · Delivery + ask

"Delivered [Y]. Here's the file. If happy, a quick review helps me a lot — and I'd love to handle [recurring task] next."

9 · Retainer pitch

"Want me to own [recurring task] monthly? $[retainer]/mo, first month 20% off. I draft the scope now."

10 · Upsell

"I can also [adjacent service] for you at $[price]. Bundle both at $[bundle], save $[save]."

11 · Handle lowball

"I don't match $5 rates — my work includes [A,B,C] and a guarantee. Happy to scope a smaller first task at $[x] so you can see quality."

12 · Portfolio ask

"Before we start, here are 3 pieces mapped to your exact need: [link1,2,3]. Which is closest to your goal?"

13 · Referral ask

"Glad [result] landed. Know one other founder who needs [service]? I'll give you both [discount] on the next project."

14 · Toptal/高端 pitch

"Senior [role] with [n] yrs delivering [outcome]. I work async, document everything, and onboard in day one. Available immediately."

15 · Objection: "we have someone"

"Understood. I'm happy to be your backup for overflow — many clients keep me for speed when their main person is booked."

16 · Weekly availability

"I have [n] hrs open this week for [service]. Booking 1–2 slots; want me to reserve one?"

17 · Case-study close

"Last month I did [result] for [client]. I can replicate that for you in [time]. Shall I send a plan?"

18 · Rush request

"I can deliver [deliverable] in [short time] for a $[rush] rush fee. Confirm and I start now."

19 · Long-term relationship

"I'd like to be your go-to for [service]. Monthly retainer, priority slots, 1 planning call/mo. Sound good?"

20 · Re-engagement

"Hi [Name] — it's been [n] weeks. I'm taking on 2 new [service] clients this month; want me to reserve a slot for your next [project]?"
