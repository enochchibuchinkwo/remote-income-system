Interview Script Pack — 20 Questions

Copy the bold line, drop in your [brackets]. Lead with proof, not apology.

1 · "Why should we hire you from Nigeria?"

"I work async by default, I've delivered for [X] clients across time zones, and my setup is fully remote-ready. My location is an advantage on cost and hours overlap with EU/US mornings."

2 · "What's your rate?"

"For this scope I work at $X/hr (or $Y fixed). I can start with a paid 1-week trial so you see output before committing."

3 · "Do you have a degree?"

"I have [certs/portfolio]. For this role the proof is in delivered work — here are three examples comparable to what you need."

4 · "Your English isn't native…"

"I write and speak in professional English daily — see my writing sample. I keep comms async and documented so nothing is lost."

5 · "What's your timezone overlap?"

"I cover [EU/US] mornings live, and I deliver async updates the rest. We'll have a daily 30-min overlap minimum."

6 · "Show me your best work."

"Here are three pieces mapped to your exact needs: [link 1] solves [problem 1], [link 2]…"

7 · "Why do you want to leave your current role?"

"I want higher-impact, dollar-denominated work and a distributed team — your opening fits exactly that."

8 · "How do you handle feedback?"

"I ship small, ask for review early, and iterate fast. Send notes in writing and I turn them around same day."

9 · "Tell me about a failure."

"I once missed a deadline from poor scoping. I now use written milestones + daily updates so it never repeats."

10 · "Are you available full-time?"

"Yes — [hours] per week, fully dedicated. I can start within 24h of approval."

11 · "How do you stay motivated remotely?"

"I run my week on outcomes, not hours. You'll see progress daily in our shared tracker."

12 · "What tools do you use?"

"Slack, Zoom, Notion, Trello, Google Workspace, [your stack]. I onboard to your stack in day one."

13 · "Can you work independently?"

"Yes — I take ownership of the deliverable end to end and flag blockers before they become problems."

14 · "Why should we trust a remote hire?"

"Trust is built with proof. I offer a paid 1-week trial so the risk is mine, not yours."

15 · "What's your notice period?"

"I can begin immediately — this is my priority engagement."

16 · "How do you communicate progress?"

"Daily async update + a 30-min weekly call. Everything in writing so nothing is assumed."

17 · "What do you need from us?"

"A clear brief, access to the tools, and one point of contact. I'll handle the rest."

18 · "Salary/rate negotiation"

"Based on the scope we discussed, $X is fair for the output. I'm open to a 3-month review once results show."

19 · "The follow-up email (after interview)"

"Hi [Name], thanks for the conversation. I'm even more excited after hearing about [specific]. I've attached one example mapped to your need. Happy to do a paid trial week — when would you like me to start?"

20 · "The trial-close (after 1 week)"

"Here's what I delivered in week one: [A, B, C]. I'd like to continue at $[rate]. Shall I send the ongoing agreement?"
