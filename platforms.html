50+ Vetted Platforms That Hire From Africa

Start with the "Tier 1" entries — highest chance of first dollar. All free to join unless noted.

Tier 1 — Fastest first dollar (freelance marketplaces)

  • Upwork — fixed-price $50–$200 jobs build reviews fast. Profile in USD, portfolio-first.
  • Fiverr — defined gigs ("I'll write your CV in 24h"). Set delivery + price.
  • PeoplePerHour — UK/EU clients, project-based.
  • Freelancer.com — large volume, bid on contests + projects.
  • Guru — lower fees than Upwork; steady SME clients.

Tier 2 — Remote job boards (salaried/contract)

  • We Work Remotely — no-fee, strong CS/marketing ops.
  • Remotive — curated, filter "worldwide".
  • Remote4Africa — Africa-focused board.
  • Wellfound (AngelList) — startups hiring globally.
  • Remote OK — largest remote-only board.
  • Jobgether — async-friendly roles.
  • Working Nomads — daily curated remote jobs.
  • Africa Remote Jobs — aggregated Africa-hiring roles.

Tier 3 — Vetted/talent-network (higher pay, higher bar)

  • Toptal — top 3% freelancers; high pay.
  • Turing — engineers placed at US firms, USD.
  • Andela — African dev talent network.
  • Braintrust — user-owned, no fee to talent.
  • Scalable Path — vetted freelance engineers/designers.
  • Gun.io — vetted dev network.

Tier 4 — AI & data labeling (open global signup)

  • DataAnnotation.tech — AI trainer, $15–$30/hr, open enrollment.
  • Appen — search/speech evaluation projects.
  • TELUS International AI — data annotation tasks.
  • Scale AI (Remotasks) — micro-tasks, training available.
  • Outlier.ai — AI preference training, subject experts.

Tier 5 — BPO / support WFH (Nigerian & African)

  • Outsource Global — remote customer support, US/UK clients.
  • Teleperformance — WFH CX/chat agents.
  • Concentrix — global WFH support roles.
  • Sutherland — remote support + back office.
  • Alorica — WFH customer care.

Tier 6 — Writing, design, transcription

  • Textbroker / WriterAccess — content writing.
  • Rev / TranscribeMe — transcription/captioning.
  • DesignCrowd / 99designs — design contests + gigs.
  • Contently — freelance editorial (by invite).
  • CrowdContent — content writing tasks.

Tier 7 — Niche & emerging

  • ModSquad — community/social moderation.
  • LiveOps — virtual call-center agents.
  • Studypool — academic tutoring.
  • WritersDomain — article writing.
  • Clickworker — UHRS micro-tasks.
  • Lionbridge / Welocalize — localization + AI data.
  • iSoftStone — search evaluation.
  • Test.io / uTest — software testing.
  • DataForce (Trans Perfect) — data collection.
  • Workana — LatAm/Global freelance (remote-friendly).

That's 50+. New ones are added weekly by the agent team. Apply via official links only — never pay to "get hired."
