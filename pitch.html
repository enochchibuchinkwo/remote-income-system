Freelance Pitch Templates (5 ready-to-send)

Replace [brackets]. Keep it short, lead with proof, offer a paid trial.

1 · Cold pitch (Upwork / Email)

"Hi [Name], I saw your post for [role]. I've delivered [result] for [client type] and can turn around [specific deliverable] in [timeframe]. I work async and send daily progress. Can I share 2 examples? Happy to do a paid 1-day trial."

2 · Proposal

"Scope: [X]. Deliverable: [Y]. Timeline: [Z]. Price: $[fixed] or $[/hr]. Includes 1 revision. I start within 24h of approval."

3 · Follow-up (no reply in 4 days)

"Hi [Name] — just circling back on my note. I can take [task] off your plate this week. Want me to send a 1-page plan so you can see the approach before deciding?"

4 · Trial offer (closes deals)

"To remove the risk, I'll do the first [deliverable] as a paid trial at $[low]. If it's not what you need, you don't continue. Most clients keep me after week one."

5 · Referral ask (after delivery)

"Glad [result] landed well. If you know one other founder who needs [service], I'll give you both a [discount] on the next project. Want me to draft the intro?"

Send these from a clean profile: real photo, USD rates, portfolio link, "Available now." Volume + proof beats perfection.
