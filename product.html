5 · Visa & Relocation Lite Guide

  • Digital Nomad visas — Portugal, Spain, Estonia, Croatia, Cape Verde offer remote-worker visas; proof of $ income required, not employer sponsorship.
  • Employer-sponsored — US H-1B / O-1, UK Skilled Worker, Canada Global Talent: your remote employer files for you after you're hired.
  • Study route — short certifications in visa-friendly countries open a foot in the door.
  • Stay-remote path — most earn dollars without ever relocating; keep Nigerian residency + USD income.
⚠️ Never pay a "relocation agent" or "visa broker" upfront. All official visas are applied for directly or via the employer. Any broker demanding fees before a job offer is a scam.
