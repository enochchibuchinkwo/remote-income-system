## The one-line strategy
Land ₦30k from Nigerians who want dollar income, using **micro-influencers + a
shareable scorecard** as the distribution engine — not paid ads, not hoping.

## 3-move playbook (copy from the $1M/mo builder)
1. **Find the obsession** — the cultural wave: "Japa", dollar income, remote work,
   freelance. Already our niche. Confirmed demand (Video 5/6).
2. **Build fast, ship ugly** — site is LIVE now. Don't wait for perfect. Launch at ₦30k,
   iterate with feedback.
3. **Distribution is everything** — see below. This is where 90% of builders fail.

## Distribution engine (run at launch + ongoing)

### A. Micro-influencer blitz (paid, cheap, high-trust)
- Target: Nigerian/Africa creators, 50k–500k followers, niches: remote work,
  freelancing, study-abroad, tech careers, side hustles, "Japa".
- Offer: free RIS access for an honest review/post, OR 20–30% rev-share per sale they drive.
- Budget: start ₦20k–₦60k per creator ($15–$40). 5–10 creators = ₦100k–₦600k test.
- Outreach: PROFESSIONAL email (not Gmail — use a domain like @remoteincomesystem.com
  via Zoho/Google Workspace). Drafted by Distribution agent.
- Track: unique discount/ref code per creator → attribute sales → pay rev-share.

### B. The "Remote Readiness Score" shareable (organic virality)
- A scorecard widget: buyer answers 5 Qs → gets a 0–100 "Readiness to earn in dollars"
  score + their "potential" score. Designed to be screenshotted + posted.
- Why it works: scorecards are inherently shareable (Umax proof). Every screenshot =
  free ad. Build into member area + a free landing-page version.
- Copy hook: "I scored 62. My potential is 89. Here's how I close the gap 👇"

### C. Faceless video + bold thumbnails (the 6-video method)
- Content agent already outputs scripts + thumbnail prompts. Produce faceless videos:
  AI visuals (FLUX) + TTS voiceover + scroll-aura editing. Post to TikTok/Shorts/Reels.
- Thumbnails: "STOP applying to these remote jobs", "You're qualified — your CV isn't",
  bold, high-contrast, red/black/yellow. Already have 2 FLUX thumbnails.
- Hook first 3 sec: a pattern interrupt ("You're applying to the WRONG sites").

### D. Problem-first spins (new SKUs)
- Mine what Africans search re: remote jobs → spin niche PDF guides as upsells
  (e.g. "Upwork First Client in 7 Days", "Visa-free remote work countries").
- Same engine, new price points (₦5k–₦15k). Feeds the funnel.
